# Natural Earth map source

`ne_110m_land.geojson` is the Natural Earth 1:110m land polygon dataset,
converted to GeoJSON by `martynafford/natural-earth-geojson`.

Natural Earth raster and vector map data are in the public domain:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

GeoJSON source:
https://github.com/martynafford/natural-earth-geojson/blob/master/110m/physical/ne_110m_land.json
